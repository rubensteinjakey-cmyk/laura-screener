I. Business plan implementation and operating results of 2025 
Amid the continued impact of geopolitical conflicts and the rise of protectionism in 2025, global 
supply chains faced significant challenges. End-market demand for consumer electronics, such as 
smartphones and personal computers, did not exhibit a notable recovery. This was further 
compounded by appreciation pressure on the New Taiwan Dollar and intensified competition in the 
large-size panel market, prompting brand customers to adopt more prudent procurement strategies. 
Leveraging precise production and sales planning as well as effective inventory management, the 
Company maintained stable annual shipment volumes. Consolidated revenue for the year reached 
NT$100.663 billion, representing a slight decrease of 2.1% compared to NT$102.788 billion of the 
previous year. However, in light of currency appreciation and rising costs of key raw materials, gross 
profit margin and operating margin declined from 40.35% and 21.21% in the prior year to 37.66% 
and 18.12%, representing decreases of 2.69 and 3.09 percentage points, respectively. Through 
disciplined cost control and improvements in operational efficiency, net income for the year amounted 
to NT$16.348 billion, with earnings per share (EPS) of NT$26.87. 
In terms of product strategy, the Company continued to optimize its product portfolio and further 
strengthened its market position in high-end notebook and smartphone segments. Benefiting from the 
global transition in display technologies, shipments of OLED display driver ICs recorded steady 
growth, underscoring the Company’s core competitiveness in advanced display technologies. At the 
same time, the Company deepened strategic collaboration with leading brand customers and achieved 
essential results, further diversifying its customer portfolio and enhancing operational resilience. 
Despite fluctuations in end-market demand and pricing pressures that affected overall profitability 
compared to the previous year, the Company demonstrated solid operational fundamentals amid 
market volatility.

(2) Circuit Design Flow  
The IC design process starts when that the design engineer converts the circuit into a

product planning, and then uses CAD and other auxiliary tools, and then manufactures it

d. Retirement system and implementation 
Novatek’s retirement system complies with the Labor Standards Act and the Labor Pension 
Act, and the Company has established the “Novatek Employee Retirement Regulations.” 
Currently, the Company follows the provisions of the Labor Standards Act to allocate 2% of 
the total monthly salary to the retirement fund, which is deposited in a special account at 
Bank of Taiwan under the name of the Labor Retirement Reserve Supervisory Committee. 
For employees who choose the Labor Pension Fund (the New Fund), 6% of their monthly 
salary is allocated to their individual retirement account at the Bureau of Labor Insurance,

[page 126]
122
and they are assisted in voluntarily contributing according to their wishes. In addition, if 
employees meet the qualifications for pension as stipulated in the “Labor Standards Act,” 
the pension is calculated based on the number of years of service and the approved average 
monthly salary at the time of retirement. For service periods of 15 years or less, two bases 
are given for each year of service, whereas for service periods exceeding 15 years, one base 
is given for each year of service, and the total number of bases must be no more than 45. The 
pension should be paid to employees within 30 days from their retirement date. 
For subsidiaries located in China, contributions are made in accordance with local 
governmental regulations. In addition to making monthly retirement contributions to 
designated government authorities, the subsidiaries also contribute pension insurance 
premiums based on a prescribed percentage of employees’ total payroll, which are deposited 
into dedicated individual accounts for each employee. Meanwhile, other overseas 
subsidiaries make retirement contributions to the relevant pension management institutions 
in compliance with applicable local laws and regulations.

a. Industry trends 
According to the December 2025 report issued by the World Semiconductor Trade Statistics 
(WSTS), the global semiconductor market is estimated to reach US$772 billion in 2025, 
representing a year-over-year growth rate of 22.0%. The forecast has been revised upward 
over the past two quarters, primarily driven by strong demand from computing-related 
applications, particularly artificial intelligence (AI)-related servers. By product category, 
growth is expected to be led by Logic (+37%) and Memory (+28%), while other segments 
such as Analog are projected to experience more modest growth (+7%). From a regional 
perspective, the strongest growth is anticipated in the Americas (+29%) and Asia-Pacific 
(+25%), while Europe is expected to record only moderate growth of approximately 6%.

Taiwan is one of the world’s major hubs in the global semiconductor industry. According to 
statistics released in the fourth quarter of 2025 by the Taiwan Semiconductor Industry 
Association (TSIA) and the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), Taiwan’s 
overall semiconductor industry output value is estimated to increase by 22.7% in 2025, 
reaching approximately NT$6.5 trillion. Among the various segments, IC manufacturing—
particularly wafer foundry services—is projected to grow by 28.5%, while IC design is 
expected to record growth of 12.0%. Looking ahead to 2026, Taiwan’s overall 
semiconductor output value is forecast to grow by 18.3%, with IC manufacturing remaining 
the primary growth driver and expected to expand by more than 20%. In contrast, IC design 
as well as IC packaging and testing are anticipated to achieve only mid-single-digit growth.

c. Product Trends 
The Group’s product line can be categorized as Display Driver ICs and System on Chips 
(SoC). 
The development of new display driver IC products must keep pace with advancements in 
panel technologies, while also meeting diverse requirements for screen sizes, resolutions, 
and image quality across various applications. In recent years, large-screen resolutions have 
continued to increase. In 2025, the penetration rate of 4K (2160 × 3840) Ultra High 
Definition (UHD) televisions approached approximately 70%, and major brands have 
successively introduced 8K televisions, with Samsung being the most active. However, due 
to relatively high pricing and limited availability of corresponding content, the penetration 
rate of 8K TVs remains at low single-digit levels. From a design perspective, product 
development efforts focus on enhancing driving capability through high-speed transmission 
interfaces, improving thermal management technologies, increasing IP and chip integration, 
lowering power consumption. In addition, emphasis is placed on delivering superior display 
performance and image quality, as well as incorporating enhanced functional integration to 
reduce the number of components and materials required on the panel side.  Through these 
efforts, the Group aims to assist customers in improving panel quality and performance, 
while simultaneously effectively reducing overall panel system costs. As a result, the total 
number of driver ICs used in UHD television panels may exceed that of Full HD panels. The 
Group continues to support customers in system integration and cost optimization, while 
enhancing electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection, achieving lower electromagnetic 
interference (EMI) requirements, improving mass production yields, product reliability, and 
consistency of product performance. These capabilities represent the core focus of future 
product and technology development. Furthermore, the adoption of OLED technology in 
mobile phone panels offers advantages such as more vivid colors and lighter, thinner displays.
