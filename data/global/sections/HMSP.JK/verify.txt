Highlights
01
With over a century of heritage, Sampoerna is the leading company in
Indonesia’s tobacco industry, with multicategory portfolio consisting of
hand-rolled cigarette, machine-made cigarette, and smoke-free products*. Our
production facilities include two machine-made kretek facilities, six hand-
rolled kretek facilities, one smoke-free tobacco products facility and 43 Third
Party Operators (TPO) for hand-rolled cigarettes, owned and managed by local
entrepreneurs and cooperatives, which contribute in creating around 90,000
job opportunities.
Rooted in our enduring Three-Hands Philosophy, we are committed to serving
adult consumers, empowering employees and business partners, delivering
value to shareholders, and contributing positively to society. Driven by
innovation and this guiding philosophy, we continue to balance stakeholder
interests, create long-term value, and grow together with Indonesia.
Sekilas Sampoerna
Sampoerna Overview
* Smoke-free products is the term Philip Morris Intnernational (PMI) uses to refer to all of its products that provide nicotine  without combusting tobacco,
such as heat-not-burn, e-Vapor  (closed-pod e-Vapor), and nicotine pouches. As such, these products have the potential to present less risk of harm
versus continued smoking.
