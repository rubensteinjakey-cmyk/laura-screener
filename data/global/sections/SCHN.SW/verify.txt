6.4 Inclusion of items on the agenda
The Board of Directors prepares the agenda of the items to be discussed. Shareholders who  represent 0.5% 
of the share capital can request that an item be included on the agenda. The request, with details of the 
item to be discussed, must be  submitted in writing by the deadline determined by the Board of Directors 
and published once in the Swiss Official Gazette of Commerce.
