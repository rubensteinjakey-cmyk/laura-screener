Tangguh di Tengah Tantangan
Saat ini, bisnis utama Perseroan berfokus pada investasi melalui 
kepemilikan pada entitas anak PT Mega Akses Persada (“FiberStar”) 
dan didukung oleh kinerja 3 (tiga) entitas asosiasi, yaitu PT Indomarco 
Prismatama (“Indomaret”), PT Fast Food Indonesia Tbk (“FAST”), dan  
PT Nippon Indosari Corpindo Tbk (“ROTI”). 
Untuk mendorong terciptanya kinerja yang solid dan tumbuh secara 
berkelanjutan, Perseroan terus berupaya menghadapi berbagai tantangan 
usaha dengan strategi yang praktikal dan taktis, seperti peningkatan 
efisiensi, pengembangan kualitas produk dan jasa, optimalisasi 
produktivitas dan efektivitas kerja, serta ekspansi yang terencana.
Currently, the Company’s core business focuses on investments through its ownership in 
the subsidiary PT Mega Akses Persada (“FiberStar”), supported by the performance of three 
associates, PT Indomarco Prismatama (“Indomaret”), PT Fast Food Indonesia Tbk (“FAST”), and 
PT Nippon Indosari Corpindo Tbk (“ROTI”).
To drive solid performance and sustainable growth, the Company continues to address 
various business challenges with practical and tactical strategies, including enhancing 
efficiency, improving product and service quality, optimizing productivity and operational 
effectiveness, and executing well-planned expansion initiatives.

Tentang Perusahaan
About the Company
PT Indoritel Makmur Internasional Tbk (the “Company”) 
is an Indonesia-based investment company with a 
business scope encompassing general trading, agency, 
and representative services. The Company began its 
business journey on November 16, 1995, under the 
name PT Dyviacom Intrabumi (“Dyviacom”), focusing as 
an Internet Service Provider (ISP) in Indonesia. A year 
later, Dyviacom officially became a prominent ISP player 
under the trademark “DNET.”
In 1998, the Company expanded its business lines by 
forming the “Dyviacom IT Solution” division. This division 
offered various technology services, ranging from 
device development and network creation, computer 
installation and supporting devices, to providing 
information system solutions, network security, 
domains, web design, and programming. This step 
served as an important foundation in strengthening the 
Company’s technical competence in its early years.
On November 21, 2000, the Company completed its 
Initial Public Offering (“IPO”) on the Indonesia Stock 
Exchange (“IDX”), offering 64 million shares at Rp250 
per share. Upon listing, the Company was formally 
registered as PT Dyviacom Intrabumi Tbk under the 
stock code DNET.
PT Indoritel Makmur Internasional Tbk (“Perseroan”) 
merupakan perusahaan investasi yang berbasis di 
Indonesia, dengan ruang lingkup usaha mencakup 
perdagangan umum, keagenan, dan perwakilan. 
Perseroan memulai perjalanan bisnisnya pada 16 
November 1995 dengan nama PT Dyviacom Intrabumi 
(“Dyviacom”), dan saat itu berfokus sebagai penyedia 
layanan Internet Service Provider  (ISP) di Indonesia. 
Setahun setelahnya, Dyviacom resmi menjadi salah satu 
pemain ISP dengan merk dagang “DNET.”
Memasuki tahun 1998, Perseroan memperluas lini 
usahanya melalui pembentukan divisi “Dyviacom IT 
Solution.” Divisi ini menawarkan berbagai layanan 
teknologi, mulai dari pengembangan perangkat dan 
pembangunan jaringan, instalasi komputer beserta 
perangkat pendukungnya, hingga penyediaan solusi 
sistem informasi, keamanan jaringan, domain, 
perancangan situs web, serta pemrograman. Langkah 
ini menjadi fondasi penting dalam memperkuat 
kompetensi teknis perusahaan pada masa awal.
Pada 21 November 2000, Perseroan resmi 
melangsungkan Penawaran Umum Perdana Saham 
(Initial Public Offering /”IPO”) di BEI dengan melepaskan 
64 juta lembar saham senilai Rp250 per lembar saham. 
Perseroan dikenal sebagai PT Dyviacom Intrabumi Tbk 
dengan kode saham DNET.

The Code of Conduct is a written guideline covering 
behavioral norms that must be obeyed and implemented 
by all employees. This guideline serves as a basic 
reference for behavior, interactions, and attitudes that 
must be obeyed by all employees in order to create a 
work environment with high ethical moral standards 
that are in line with the Company’s cultural values.
The Company has a Code of Conduct that was prepared 
based on GCG principles and prevailing laws and 
regulations. The formulation of the Company’s Code 
of Conduct has been set forth in the Collective Labor 
Agreement (PKB), which is signed by employees when 
they first join the Company. With the existence of the 
Code of Conduct, the Company is committed to ensuring 
that business management and the achievement of 
company performance are always carried out in ethical 
and moral ways and do not violate applicable statutory 
regulations.
Principles of the Code of Conduct
The Code of Conduct covers the foundations for the 
management of operations, business, and relationships 
with shareholders and stakeholders, including:
1. Protection of the Company’s assets;
2. Anti-corruption ethics;
3. Fair treatment;
4. Ethical behaviors in the workplace includes moral 
standards and integrity; personal interests; business 
activities in other places; kinship relationships; 
discrimination or harassment; safety, health, and 
security in the workplace; workplace free of illegal 
drugs; communications, equipment, and service 
systems; information and investigation;
5. Ethics related to conflict of interest which includes 
gifts and entertainment; political activities and 
contributions; ownership; as well as the use of 
facilities and company name.
Statement of Code of Conduct Applies to 
All Organizational Levels
The Company’s Code of Conduct applies to all 
employees at every level of the organization, without 
exception. This includes both contract and permanent 
employees, as well as core management, namely the 
Board of Commissioners and Directors. The universal 
application of the Code of Ethics reflects the Company’s 
commitment to fostering a positive and productive work 
environment.
Kode Etik Perseroan [IDX G.07]
Company Code of Conduct
Kode Etik merupakan pedoman tertulis mencakup 
norma-norma perilaku yang wajib dipatuhi dan 
dilaksanakan oleh seluruh karyawan. Pedoman ini 
berfungsi sebagai acuan dasar berperilaku, berinteraksi, 
dan bersikap yang wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh karyawan 
agar tercipta lingkungan kerja dengan standar moral 
beretika tinggi yang selaras dengan nilai-nilai dan 
budaya Perseroan.
Perseroan memiliki Kode Etik yang disusun berdasarkan 
prinsip-prinsip GCG dan peraturan perundang-
undangan yang berlaku. Rumusan Kode Etik Perseroan 
telah dituangkan dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) 
yang ditandatangani oleh karyawan pada saat pertama 
kali bergabung di Perseroan. Dengan berpegang pada 
Kode Etik, Perseroan memastikan pengelolaan bisnisnya 
benar-benar dilakukan dengan kaidah-kaidah etika dan 
moralitas yang tepat serta mematuhi ketentuan dan 
perundang-undangan yang berlaku.
Pokok-Pokok Kode Etik
Kode Etik memuat landasan-landasan terkait 
pengelolaan operasional, bisnis, dan hubungan 
dengan pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan, 
termasuk di antaranya:
1. Perlindungan terhadap aset-aset perusahaan;
2. Etika antikorupsi;
3. Perlakuan adil;
4. Etika berperilaku di tempat kerja mencakup standar 
moral dan integritas; kepentingan pribadi; kegiatan 
bisnis di tempat lain; hubungan kekerabatan; 
diskriminasi atau pelecehan; keselamatan, 
kesehatan dan keamanan di tempat kerja; tempat 
kerja bebas dari obat-obatan terlarang; komunikasi, 
peralatan, dan sistem layanan; informasi dan 
penyelidikan;
5. Etika terkait konflik kepentingan yang meliputi 
hadiah dan perjamuan; kegiatan dan kontribusi 
politik; kepemilikan; serta penggunaan fasilitas dan 
nama perusahaan.
Pernyataan Kode Etik Berlaku untuk 
Seluruh Level Organisasi
Seluruh karyawan dari semua lapisan organisasi terikat 
dengan Kode Etik Perseroan tanpa terkecuali, termasuk 
pegawai kontrak maupun tetap, hingga ke jajaran 
manajemen inti yaitu Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. 
Universalitas pemberlakuan Kode Etik merupakan 
bentuk komitmen Perseroan dalam mewujudkan 
lingkungan kerja yang kondusif dan konstruktif.

CHALLENGES IN THE IMPLEMENTATION 
OF SUSTAINABILITY PRACTICES [POJK A.5e]
The effective implementation of sustainability 
practices requires awareness, understanding, and 
active participation from all employees in executing 
sustainable initiatives. Internally, the Company 
acknowledges varying levels of knowledge, competence, 
and understanding among employees regarding the 
application of environmental, social, and governance 
(ESG) principles in daily operations. These disparities 
have limited the consistent execution of the Company’s 
sustainability strategy across all organizational functions.
To address this issue, the Company has taken a more 
proactive approach by expanding the socialization of 
sustainability initiatives and providing various training 
programs relevant to sustainable business practices. 
These efforts are aimed at building a more uniform 
understanding and enhancing employees’ capabilities 
in implementing ESG principles. Despite these internal 
challenges, the Company remains optimistic that 
through improved literacy, awareness, and employee 
engagement, the effectiveness of the sustainability 
strategy will continue to improve over time.

value indicates more efficient energy use. In 2025,

Tinjauan Perekonomian
Economy Review
Perekonomian Indonesia pada tahun 2025 menunjukkan 
kinerja yang tetap tangguh di tengah dinamika global, 
dengan pertumbuhan mencapai sekitar 5,11% yang 
didorong oleh kuatnya permintaan domestik, khususnya 
konsumsi rumah tangga dan investasi. Konsumsi rumah 
tangga, yang berkontribusi lebih dari separuh terhadap 
Produk Domestik Bruto, tumbuh stabil seiring terjaganya 
daya beli masyarakat, inflasi yang relatif terkendali di 
kisaran rendah, serta berbagai stimulus pemerintah 
yang mendorong aktivitas ekonomi dan mobilitas 
masyarakat. Kondisi ini mencerminkan fundamental 
ekonomi yang solid, meskipun tetap dibayangi oleh 
tantangan eksternal seperti ketidakpastian global, 
fluktuasi nilai tukar, dan dinamika harga komoditas.

[page 90]
Sejalan dengan kondisi makro tersebut, sektor ritel dan 
industri makanan dan minuman (F&B) tetap menjadi 
salah satu motor utama pertumbuhan konsumsi 
domestik. Komponen konsumsi makanan dan minuman 
menyumbang porsi terbesar dalam belanja rumah 
tangga dan terus mencatat pertumbuhan positif, 
didorong oleh peningkatan mobilitas masyarakat, 
perubahan gaya hidup, serta penetrasi kanal distribusi 
modern dan digital. Selain itu, percepatan digitalisasi 
pembayaran dan ekspansi platform e- commerce turut 
memperkuat kinerja ritel, terutama pada segmen fast 
moving consumer goods  (FMCG). Namun demikian, 
industri ini juga menghadapi tekanan berupa perubahan 
pola konsumsi yang lebih selektif, persaingan harga 
yang ketat, serta fluktuasi biaya bahan baku.

[page 91]
On the other hand, the telecommunications 
infrastructure industry, particularly fiber-optic 
networks, has experienced significant growth, laying 
the foundation for Indonesia’s digital economy. Faster 
internet penetration, the adoption of 5G technology, 
and the demand for high-speed data services have 
accelerated the nationwide deployment of fiber-optic 
networks. As of 2025, fiber-optic networks in Indonesia 
have expanded rapidly, reaching hundreds of thousands 
of kilometers, with continued expansion by both the 
government and the private sector to cover broader 
regions. The coming years will see continued growth 
in fiber-based fixed broadband services (FTTH) as the 
digital connectivity needs of households and businesses 
continue to expand. Sustained investment from 
industry players reflects a positive long-term outlook, 
with fiber-optic infrastructure increasingly positioned 
as the backbone of digital transformation and national 
economic competitiveness.

[page 92]
The Company, through its subsidiary FiberStar, operated 
as a provider of fiber optic–based infrastructure and 
delivered telecommunications services, including data 
communication, voice, interactive video, managed 
SD-WAN services, and various Internet of Things (IoT) 
derivatives. FiberStar’s customers comprised Internet 
Service Providers (ISPs), cellular telecommunications 
operators (cellular provider), and enterprises across 
multiple segments, including banking, insurance, 
financial services, higher education, hospitality, retail, 
and convenience stores.

[page 94]
Dalam lingkup nasional, pemerintah juga terus berupaya 
mengakselerasi aktivitas ekonomi melalui berbagai 
program stimulus menjelang akhir tahun. Menyambut 
libur akhir tahun dan periode Lebaran, sejumlah insentif 
akan digulirkan, antara lain diskon transportasi pada 
22 Desember 2025 hingga 10 Januari 2026, program 
belanja nasional EPIC Sale dengan target transaksi Rp56 
triliun, Harbolnas dengan sasaran Rp34 triliun, serta 
program BINA dengan target Rp30 triliun. Rangkaian 
kebijakan tersebut diharapkan mampu mendorong 
belanja masyarakat sekaligus menjaga keberlanjutan 
momentum konsumsi domestik.

[page 94]
Prospek kinerja emiten di sektor barang konsumsi cepat 
saji (FMCG) dan ritel diproyeksikan semakin positif pada 
2026, seiring dengan penguatan daya beli masyarakat. 
Kondisi konsumen Indonesia diperkirakan berada pada 
level yang lebih baik, tercermin dari indeks keyakinan 
konsumen yang konsisten berada di zona optimistis 
selama 48 bulan berturut-turut. Analis menilai, kenaikan 
pendapatan riil di berbagai sektor padat karya menjadi 
pendorong utama permintaan rumah tangga, yang 
pada akhirnya berdampak pada meningkatnya daya 
beli. Selain itu, adanya ruang pelonggaran kebijakan 
moneter, terutama penurunan BI Rate, turut berpotensi 
memberikan stimulus tambahan bagi pertumbuhan 
konsumsi.

[page 94]
The government projects that economic growth 
prospects for 2026 will remain solid, with a target of 
5.4%. This optimism is supported by several strategic 
developments in the international trade agenda that are 
further strengthening the economic foundation for the 
future. The ongoing process of finalizing cooperation 
with the United States is a significant milestone and 
marks the near completion of the entire 2025 trade 
agenda. Additionally, Indonesia has concluded the IEU-
CEPA negotiations, which are targeted to take effect in 
2027. Significant progress has also been recorded in the 
OECD accession process, which is becoming increasingly 
tangible and has received broad support from various 
countries, with the expectation that Indonesia will 
officially become an OECD member by 2027.
