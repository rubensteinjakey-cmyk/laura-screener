Our investment plan for New York includes construction of a leading-edge DRAM memory manufacturing site, consisting of up to four fabs to be built over the next 20-plus years, in Clay, New York. In January 2026, we broke ground on our first New York fab. In July 2026, we achieved the first concrete pour for the foundation of that fab, marking the transition from site preparation into vertical construction. We expect initial wafer output in calendar 2030. We expect these new fabs to be key to meeting our requirements for additional wafer capacity, in line with industry demand trends and our objective of maintaining stable bit share.
