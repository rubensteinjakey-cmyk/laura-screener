Geopolitical tensions and broader macroeconomic pressures have contributed, and may continue to contribute, to wage inflation, labor availability challenges, product cost increases (including the impact of tariffs), and supply chain disruptions. Additionally, adverse weather events, climate change, and other unforeseen circumstances outside of our control may strain our supply chain, potentially limiting our ability to source necessary products and adversely affecting consumer spending patterns. We continually monitor our operating environment and remain prepared to adapt our capital allocation strategy, including share repurchase activity, in response to evolving market conditions. Our ongoing priorities include strong cash flow generation and preserving a sound, flexible financial foundation to support the long-term execution of our business investment strategy.
