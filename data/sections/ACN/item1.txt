Item 1. Business

Overview

Accenture is a business reinvention and technology company. Our ambition is to be the reinvention partner of choice for our clients and lead in the safe, widespread adoption of AI, and to be the most client-focused, AI-enabled, great place to work in the world. Our people bring deep insights across industries and functions, with capabilities spanning strategy, process, operating model, change management, talent, data, technology and AI. We deliver this expertise through consulting, managed services, proprietary assets and platforms, and increasingly, software. We bring innovation and new solutions informed by our experience across industries, enabling us to apply relevant insights, capabilities and leading practices from adjacent sectors to address emerging business challenges and opportunities. We serve more than 9,000 clients and generated revenues of approximately $74 billion in fiscal year 2026.

Reinvention Services

Reinvention Services brings together our ecosystem partner relationships, assets and platforms and our network of businesses, utilizing our Reinvention Partners and Reinvention Engines.

Reinvention Partners

Our seven Reinvention Partners are organized around how our clients operate, make decisions and buy services. Each Reinvention Partner aligns our end-to-end capabilities to a principal enterprise function, buyer or area of the industry value chain. This structure allows us to more easily and repeatably deliver business outcomes, embed the latest developments in data, AI, technology, assets and platforms, and scale reinvention deeper in a function and broader across the enterprise. Our Reinvention Partners increase C-suite access and provide new growth opportunities by focusing on client buyers and the outcomes they need.

Cybersecurity – helps clients reinvent cyber-resilience and value through trust—building defenses, protecting enterprises, managing risk, and enabling the secure adoption of emerging technologies, AI, data, secure AI, cloud and digital platforms.

Digital Core – helps clients reinvent their digital foundations—including technology strategy and architecture, AI, data, cloud, application modernization and management, digital infrastructure, and platform services—to create value at speed and scale.

Finance – helps clients reinvent financial performance and enterprise operations by supporting the CFO agenda, driving best-in-class performance, enabling insights and benchmarking, and transforming finance, accounting, and related processes through AI-powered, cloud-enabled capabilities.

Industry and Enterprise – helps clients reinvent core industry value chains and drive end-to-end, cross-functional reinvention intended to deliver growth and sustained enterprise value. Industry and Enterprise partners on reinvention across industries in Financial Services, Resources, Products, Health & Public Service and Communications, Media & Technology.

Song – helps clients reinvent how they grow by focusing on the end-to-end customer agenda—bringing together customer growth strategy, marketing, sales, service, commerce, design, digital products, AI and data to create customer-led growth.

Ecosystem Partner Relationships

Our network of strong ecosystem partnerships, including our key technology partners, is important to our growth. Our partners come to us for help to drive adoption by enterprises, turn their technology into business outcomes and scale the adoption of AI. As the AI and data ecosystem continues to rapidly evolve, we have expanded these relationships and added new AI and data partnerships to expand our capabilities. Our key 18 ecosystem partners represented approximately 65% of fiscal 2026 revenue, with growth outpacing Accenture overall.

Assets and Platforms

We bring industry-specific solutions and services, as well as cross industry expertise, and we leverage our scale and global footprint, innovation capabilities, and strong ecosystem partnerships including our proprietary assets and platforms, such as GenWizard, SynOps and reinvention.AI, to deliver tangible value for our clients.

Network of Businesses

We are investing in a network of businesses focused on attractive growth areas. These businesses expand our capabilities in selected markets and are designed to operate with commercial and operating models tailored to their respective market opportunities. At the same time, each business is connected to our Reinvention Partners, described above, and benefits from Accenture’s client relationships, industry expertise, relationships across the technology ecosystem, investment capacity and global scale. Our network of businesses represented approximately 6% of our revenue in FY26.

The network currently includes businesses focused on areas such as operational technology security (through our acquisition of Dragos), AI and decision intelligence (through our acquisition of Faculty), capital projects (through the launch of Accenture Construct), data centers (through our majority acquisition of DLB), education (through Accenture LearnVantage), data (through our acquisition of Ookla), health (through our acquisition of the digital health platform Alfahealth), and mid-market solutions (through Avanade and our launch of Accenture Edge). We expect to continue investing in and scaling these businesses and may pursue additional areas where this model provides a competitive advantage.

Competition

Accenture operates in a highly competitive and rapidly changing global marketplace. We compete with a variety of organizations that offer solutions and services competitive with those we offer—but we believe no other company offers the full range of services at scale that Accenture does, which uniquely positions us in a highly competitive market. Our clients typically retain us on a non-exclusive basis.

Our competitors include large multinational IT service providers, including the services arms of large global technology providers; offshore IT service providers in lower-cost locations, particularly in India; accounting firms and consultancies that provide consulting, managed services and other IT solutions and services; solution or service providers that compete with us in a specific geographic market, industry or service area, including advertising agency holding companies, engineering services providers and technology start-ups, AI-native companies and software companies that can scale rapidly to focus on or disrupt certain markets and provide new or alternative products, end-to-end solutions, services, or commercial or delivery models; and in-house IT departments of large corporations that use their own resources rather than engage an outside firm, such as global capability centers (“GCCs”). Our ecosystem partners also offer solutions and services that compete with some of our solutions and services.
