In our consulting business, revenues for fiscal 2026 increased 5% in U.S. dollars and 3% in local currency compared to fiscal 2025. Consulting revenue growth in local currency for fiscal 2026 was driven by very strong growth in Asia Pacific, solid growth in the Americas and slight growth in EMEA. Our consulting revenue continues to be driven by helping our clients accelerate their reinvention and drive business outcomes, by redesigning processes and operating models, modernizing and implementing technology and data foundations, and leveraging cloud, software enterprise platforms, security, AI and data, as well as our change capabilities to help clients build new skills and drive the successful adoption of new processes and technologies. In addition, clients continue to be focused on initiatives designed to deliver cost savings, supply chain and operational resilience, as well as to accelerate growth and improve customer experiences. While we continue to experience demand for these services, particularly large transformation programs, the discretionary spending environment continues to impact smaller contracts with a shorter duration.
